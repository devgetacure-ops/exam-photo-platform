# Product journey: slide purpose

The sequence is designed for a busy applicant. Every slide answers one question and shows the relevant product mechanism.

| Slide | Reader question | Product proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What is this, and how fast can I understand it? | One route from exam search to ready files. |
| 2 | How do I begin? | Search by exam name or short form; the exam record drives the experience. |
| 3 | Does it know what my form actually needs? | Supported files and portal-only steps are separated; rules and sources remain visible. |
| 4 | Can I choose only what applies to me? | Required and optional files can be selected; the price follows the kit. |
| 5 | What happens after upload? | File-specific preparation uses the selected exam's dimensions, size, format and filename rules. |
| 6 | How do I judge the result? | Protected preview, plain-language checks and regeneration after a retry. |
| 7 | What happens when I pay? | Server quote, Razorpay, clean-file unlock, downloads, email and reminder link. |
| 8 | What are the limits and privacy terms? | Source/estimate markers, portal authority, identity preservation, no training and short retention. |

Reddit condenses the same journey to six cards. X condenses it to four paired stages. No platform receives a random subset of screenshots.
