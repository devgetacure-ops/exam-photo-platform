## Product-overview sequence

1. Define the product in one sentence.
2. Explain why a generic resize tool is insufficient.
3. Show the complete set of supported application-file categories.
4. Establish the exam-first starting point and current examination coverage.
5. Show the supported-versus-portal-only distinction on a real examination.
6. Show candidate control over the selected kit.
7. Explain protected review before payment.
8. State pricing and PDF inclusion.
9. State account and file-retention boundaries.
10. Present the installable mobile application with real phone views.
11. Close on the product promise and URL.

The LinkedIn document and Instagram deck use all eleven points. Reddit removes
the three most marketing-oriented frames. X compresses the argument into four
images: problem, exam-first method, proof before payment and the installable
application.
