## Accuracy boundaries

- Say `prepared to the recorded requirements`, never `guaranteed accepted`.
- State that the candidate submits the final file on the official portal.
- Use examination and cycle names when discussing a specific rule.
- Do not turn an estimate into a published requirement.
- The examples on the site are representational and use generated people or
  invented signatures. They are not candidate files or engine-output proof.
- Recheck live pricing, examination count and retention language before posting
  if the campaign is published after launch day.
