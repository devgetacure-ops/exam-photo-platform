# ExamUploadKit launch sequence

## Before publishing

1. Confirm the homepage, examination search and one complete preparation flow
   on both phone and desktop.
2. Confirm the live price, temporary-file window and current examination count.
   Do not place those changing values inside artwork unless re-rendered that day.
3. Add the canonical product description from `copy.md` to each product profile.
4. Use a distinct tagged link for Instagram, LinkedIn, X and Reddit so visits can
   be attributed without changing the destination experience.
5. Prepare one person to answer comments during the first two hours.

## Instagram launch

Publish the pinned set close together in this order:

1. `KIT / WHAT`
2. `UPLOAD / HOW`
3. `EXAM / WHY`

The newest post normally appears first in the grid, so this reverse publication
order is intended to produce `EXAM · UPLOAD · KIT` from left to right. Pin all
three and inspect the profile immediately; correct the order manually if the
account's current grid behaviour differs.

Each carousel stands alone, but the pinned row reads as one identity:

- `EXAM` creates recognition around the problem.
- `UPLOAD` makes the method understandable.
- `KIT` resolves the story with the candidate's outcome.

Post the personal Story and WhatsApp Status after the three carousels are live,
so every referral lands on a complete profile rather than an unfinished row.

## Public platform sequence

### Launch day

- **LinkedIn:** publish the editorial card with the full founder-style post from
  the product account. Stay available to answer specific product questions.
- **X:** publish the visual with post 1, then add the four replies as one thread.
- **Instagram:** publish and pin the three-carousels set, then share the personal
  Story from the personal account.
- **WhatsApp:** use the four Status frames and a direct link in the final frame.

### Days 2–4

- Post to one suitable Reddit community at a time. Adapt the title and opening
  to its rules; do not repeat the same post across communities in one burst.
- Turn the best real question into a short answer post from the product account.
- Share one specific correction or improvement made from launch feedback.

### Days 5–7

- Repost the strongest carousel slide to Story with a question sticker asking
  which exam or rule is hardest to handle.
- Publish one rule-specific explainer only after checking the applicable source.
- Collect recurring confusion into product and content priorities.

## Response guide

Reply to praise briefly, then ask whether the person knows an active candidate.
Reply to criticism with the examination name, the step affected and what will be
checked. Never debate a rule from memory. Ask for the official notice or portal
reference when the stored requirement may be wrong.

## Measures that matter

- People who reach examination search from each platform.
- Searches that return no matching examination.
- Starts and completions of the preparation flow.
- Shares and saves, especially when followed by a qualified comment.
- Rule corrections or missing examinations reported by candidates.

Treat raw impressions as reach, not proof that the message or product worked.
