# ExamUploadKit launch campaign — dark editorial direction

## Campaign idea

**The last part of the exam nobody prepares you for.**

The campaign begins with a familiar emotional truth: candidates prepare for the
exam, then lose time and confidence at an upload screen asking for exact pixel
dimensions and file-size limits. ExamUploadKit makes that last, avoidable piece
orderly.

The work should feel like an editorial campaign about pressure, precision and
relief. It should not look like product documentation, a UI tutorial, a generic
technology launch or a collection of website screenshots.

## Three pinned carousels

The covers retain the authorised logo excerpts exactly:

1. `EXAM` — **WHY**: the hidden upload problem and why it matters.
2. `UPLOAD` — **HOW**: the exam-first system that removes guesswork.
3. `KIT` — **WHAT**: the files candidates receive and the relief that creates.

Each cover uses a different original dark image. Together they move from
pressure to precision to calm.

## Art direction

- Deep graphite, smoked black and warm off-white.
- Vermilion orange used as a signal, never as decoration.
- Hard directional light for the problem.
- Controlled raking light for the system.
- Soft resolved light for the benefit.
- The materials feel physical. Paper fibres and smoked glass sit beside
  blackened metal. Human hands keep the precision grounded.
- Large condensed typography with disciplined alignment.
- Copy limited to one thought per slide.
- The logo is always composited from the authorised asset after image
  generation. It is never generated, redrawn or recoloured.

## Visual progression

### EXAM / WHY

A candidate's late-night desk. The exam preparation is substantial; the upload
failure is absurdly small. Composition feels tense and slightly unresolved.

### UPLOAD / HOW

A physical precision system: source material passes through crop frames,
measurement gates and format controls. Nothing looks magical. Everything looks
deliberate.

### KIT / WHAT

The prepared artifacts sit in a custom portfolio tray. The composition is calm,
ordered and complete. The emotional promise is relief, not technical novelty.

## Platform treatments

- **Instagram:** narrative carousel, high visual pacing, one idea per slide.
- **Personal Story / WhatsApp:** emotional first-person message with a quiet
  reveal; no development timeline.
- **LinkedIn:** maker-led editorial launch image with the problem stated in one
  sentence. The post carries the explanation.
- **X:** one arresting visual contrast and a short thread. Fast enough to
  understand without opening the thread.
- **Reddit:** text-first, transparent self-promotion and community-specific
  questions. No ad-looking image unless the community format rewards one.

## Avoid

- Product screenshots as the main visual idea.
- Reusing website comparison assets.
- Fake official portals or readable government forms.
- Glowing blue technology imagery.
- Floating UI cards and generic SaaS diagrams.
- Stock candidates smiling at laptops.
- Dense slide copy, feature inventories or inflated claims.
