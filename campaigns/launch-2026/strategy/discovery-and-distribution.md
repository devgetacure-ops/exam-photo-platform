# Discovery and distribution plan

## Identity boundary

- Personal WhatsApp and personal Instagram story may use the owner's personal
  voice.
- Public Instagram, LinkedIn, X and Reddit activity uses the ExamUploadKit name.
- Public maker copy may say `I built`, but does not identify the maker, employer,
  job title, personal account or development timeline.

## Search language

Use these phrases only where they describe the page or post naturally:

- exam photo size
- photo resize for exam form
- signature resize for exam form
- compress photo to KB for exam form
- exam application upload requirements
- photograph and signature size
- thumb impression size for exam form
- handwritten declaration format

Do not paste the same phrase set into every caption. Each post should own one
candidate question and link to the page that answers it.

## SEO

1. Link each exam-specific post to that examination's page, not always to the
   homepage.
2. Use the exact examination name and cycle only when the linked record supports
   it.
3. Keep titles, descriptions, headings, visible copy and alt text aligned around
   the same intent.
4. Track launch links with stable campaign parameters while keeping the
   canonical page URL unchanged.
5. Review Search Console queries after launch and promote actual demand instead
   of inferred keyword volume.

## AEO

Each useful post should answer one question in a form that can stand on its own:

1. Direct answer in the first two sentences.
2. Applicable examination and cycle.
3. Exact rule value or constraint.
4. Link to the official source through the ExamUploadKit rule page.
5. Clear statement of what the product prepares and what remains the
   candidate's responsibility.

## GEO

No separate trick is required. The content should be easy to quote accurately:

- consistent product name and description
- concise factual paragraphs
- visible source links and checked dates
- no guaranteed-acceptance language
- no unsupported comparisons or invented proof
- the same price, catalogue count and privacy facts everywhere they appear.

## Distribution sequence

### Launch day

1. Pin the three Instagram carousels in the visual order `EXAM | UPLOAD | KIT`.
2. Publish the personal WhatsApp status and Instagram story with the product
   link.
3. Publish the LinkedIn launch from the ExamUploadKit identity.
4. Stay available to answer replies and capture confusing language.

### Day 2

1. Publish the X launch thread with a short real-product recording.
2. Share the `UPLOAD` carousel to stories with a direct link.
3. Turn the first repeated question into a plain answer post.

### Days 3–5

1. Post one Reddit version only after checking that community's current rules,
   flair and self-promotion policy.
2. Rewrite the opening and question for that community. Do not cross-post an
   identical body into several subreddits.
3. Answer every serious comment without pretending to be an unaffiliated user.

### Week 2

Publish the first exam-specific answer post based on actual searches, comments
or support questions. Link to the applicable rule page.

## Measurement

Track:

- visits by source and campaign
- exam selections
- preparation starts and completions
- checkout starts and confirmed payments
- missing-exam searches
- substantive comments, saves and shares
- Search Console impressions and clicks over the following weeks.

Do not publish conversion claims or user counts until the product has enough
real data to support them.
