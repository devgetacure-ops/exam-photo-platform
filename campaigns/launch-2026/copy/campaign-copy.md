## LinkedIn

I built a small product for a frustratingly specific problem: exam application
uploads.

A candidate may need a photograph in one set of dimensions, a signature within
a narrow file-size range, a thumb impression in another format, and a
declaration prepared differently again. The requirements can change with the
examination and cycle. A generic image resizer does not know any of that.

ExamUploadKit starts with the examination. It shows the published upload rules
we have confirmed, prepares the files it supports, and gives the candidate a

## Reddit: candidate community version

### Title

I built a tool that prepares exam photos, signatures and other upload files to
the exam's published rules

### Body

I kept running into the same awkward problem in exam application forms: the
photograph has one dimension and file-size rule, the signature has another, and
some forms also ask for a thumb impression, declaration or document scan.

So I built ExamUploadKit. You choose the examination first. The site shows the
upload requirements we have confirmed from published sources and prepares the
files it supports to those recorded specifications.

It is live at https://examuploadkit.com.

To be transparent, this is my product. One chargeable file currently costs ₹3;
a kit with two or more is capped at ₹5. [Launch-day check: confirm both prices.]
It does not submit the application or guarantee acceptance by the authority.

I would appreciate criticism from people who are actually filling exam forms:
which requirement is still confusing, which exam is missing, and where does the
flow waste your time?

## Reddit: Indian builder/product community version

### Title

I built an exam-first file-preparation product instead of another generic photo
resizer

### Body

I have launched ExamUploadKit, a small product for Indian exam application
uploads.

The product begins with a structured examination record. That record determines
the photograph rules and the signature, thumb impression, declaration or
document work shown to the candidate. The output is prepared for download; the
candidate submits it on the authority's portal.

The constraint I cared about most was refusing to guess. If a published rule is
missing or ambiguous, the product must say that rather than turn a convenient
default into a false claim.

The live catalogue currently contains 770 examination records. One chargeable
file costs ₹3, and a kit with two or more is capped at ₹5. [Launch-day check the
count and both prices.]

Site: https://examuploadkit.com

I am looking for criticism of the exam-selection flow, the explanation of
requirements, and whether the trust boundary is clear. I am posting as the
maker of the product.
