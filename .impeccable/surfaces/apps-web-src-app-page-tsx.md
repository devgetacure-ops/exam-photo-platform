## Direction contract

THESIS: The examination's own form, reclaimed as an interface. This page owns the
device every Indian candidate already knows by heart — the letter-box grid you
write one character into, the dashed photo-affix square, PART A and PART B — and
turns the thing that frustrates them into the thing that orients them. It refuses
the category default for this product: a centred wordmark over a soft-shadow card
grid with a gradient hero and three feature tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white #FAF9F6, near-black #111110, one signal #F04E23 and its
dark-theme warm #FF6A3D. Hard 3px borders, flat fills, offset solid shadows, zero
radius, zero blur, zero gradient. Big Shoulders Display 900 condensed for display,
Courier Prime for labels and measurements, Anek Latin for body, Petrona italic for
asides only. The wordmark is thirteen connected cells sharing 1px dividers inside
one outer border — the same construction at every size. One surface, and only one,
carries a faint 40px grid.

STORY: A candidate mid-application understands that every examination publishes a
different specification, believes we have actually read theirs, and searches for it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header, wordmark left as connected cells, Courier nav right. Hero
splits: 452px left column with an 88px four-line display line, "the files" in a
solid signal block, a Petrona italic standfirst and one body paragraph; right, two
overlapping flat prints — as-uploaded and prepared — rotated -4deg and +2.5deg with
hard offset shadows, the prepared one bordered in signal and carrying its real
dimensions and byte size. Below both, full-width, the primary action: a bordered
search bar with a 7px offset shadow and a solid ₹3 block closing its right edge.

FORM: The form-document world, first on the ordered list and pinned by the owner
across two direction rounds rather than dealt — no seed key; the selection is
recorded in the direction board's two pages.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
