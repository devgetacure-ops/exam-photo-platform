# Indian Exam Registration Deliverables Report

**Evidence cut-off:** 5 August 2026  
**Coverage:** 48 distinct examination/recruitment identities represented by 50 stage-specific records  
**Purpose:** Identify what a candidate must prepare, create, capture, scan, upload, write, or complete during registration/application.

> This report deliberately excludes product architecture, pricing, software engines, security, privacy, governance, compliance and business planning. It focuses only on candidate registration deliverables.

## 1. Executive summary

The registration deliverables across the covered examinations fall into a small number of recurring types: candidate photograph, live photograph, signature, thumb/finger impression, handwritten declaration, identity proof, qualification proof, category/disability certificate, and a portal declaration. The important differences are exam-specific: whether an item is uploaded or captured live, whether it is mandatory for everyone or conditional, the exact file specification, and the stage at which it is required.

The report does not invent a universal checklist. A field is marked **not found** or **portal-dependent** where the accessible official material did not establish it. Prior-cycle and secondary-only values remain labelled as such.

## 2. Scope and reading rules

- **Mandatory** means every candidate in the stated route must provide the item.
- **Conditional** means it is required only when the candidate claims a category, concession, accommodation, eligibility route or special status.
- **Official live capture** is not an ordinary upload; the candidate must complete it inside the official portal/application.
- **Physical-stage requirement** is shown separately and must not be mistaken for a registration upload.
- When an exact file value is missing, the report says so rather than substituting a generic value.

## 1. UPSC Civil Services Examination 2026

- **Conducting body:** Union Public Service Commission
- **Cycle:** 2026
- **Stage / record:** application / candidate_photograph
- **Evidence source for photograph record:** https://upsconline.nic.in/ngrp/assets/PDF/instruction-photo-signature-upload-upsc.pdf

### Complete registration deliverables

| Deliverable | Status | Submission method | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Latest passport-size photograph | Mandatory | `file_upload` | All applicants |
| Live photograph | Mandatory | `official_live_capture` | All applicants |
| Triple-signature image | Mandatory | `handwritten_then_uploaded` | All applicants |
| Application declaration | Mandatory | `typed_or_selected_declaration` | All applicants |

### Detailed deliverable specifications

#### 1. Latest passport-size photograph

- **Requirement status:** Mandatory
- **Submission method:** `file_upload`
- **Applicability:** All applicants
- **Specification:** JPG; 20-200 KB; exact filename 'photo.jpg'. Colour, recent, plain white background, frontal full face, head centred, both ears visible, eyes open, natural expression, at least 75% face coverage; no shadows, dark glasses, uniform or signed photograph.
- **Evidence status:** Current official UPSC common application instruction
- **Source:** https://upsconline.nic.in/ngrp/assets/PDF/instruction-photo-signature-upload-upsc.pdf

#### 2. Live photograph

- **Requirement status:** Mandatory
- **Submission method:** `official_live_capture`
- **Applicability:** All applicants
- **Specification:** Captured during the UPSC application using the current device webcam or by scanning the displayed QR code and using a mobile device. Good lighting and clear face required. The live image is matched with the uploaded passport photograph; mismatch blocks further application.
- **Evidence status:** Current official UPSC common application instruction
- **Source:** https://upsconline.nic.in/ngrp/assets/PDF/instruction-photo-signature-upload-upsc.pdf

#### 3. Triple-signature image

- **Requirement status:** Mandatory
- **Submission method:** `handwritten_then_uploaded`
- **Applicability:** All applicants
- **Specification:** Sign three times vertically, one below another, on plain white paper using black ink. Scan all three signatures in one clear, correctly oriented image. JPG; filename 'signature.jpg'; 20-100 KB; published image dimension 350-500 pixels.
- **Evidence status:** Current official UPSC common application instruction
- **Source:** https://upsconline.nic.in/ngrp/assets/PDF/instruction-photo-signature-upload-upsc.pdf

#### 4. Application declaration

- **Requirement status:** Mandatory
- **Submission method:** `typed_or_selected_declaration`
- **Applicability:** All applicants
- **Specification:** Candidate confirms the correctness of the application before final submission. No separate uploaded declaration file was found in the common instruction.
- **Evidence status:** Portal step; exact wording varies by application
- **Source:** https://upsconline.nic.in/

### Rejection and failure conditions

- Shadows on the face or background are not accepted.
- Non-frontal or side-facing pose can fail the stated requirement.
- Eyes must be visible/open as stated.
- Spectacle glare, dark or coloured glasses can make the photograph unacceptable.
- Face coverage below 75%, blurred image, misaligned face, face too close/out of frame, or ear lobes not visible are explicit UPSC rejection examples.
- A live-photo mismatch with the uploaded passport photograph prevents the candidate from proceeding.
- A signature image that is not exactly three vertically arranged signatures, is blurred/dark, incorrectly oriented, or outside the published file specification is rejected.

- **Latest passport-size photograph** - JPG; 20-200 KB; exact filename 'photo.jpg'. Colour, recent, plain white background, frontal full face, head centred, both ears visible, eyes open, natural expression, at least 75% face coverage; no shadows, dark glasses, uniform or signed photograph.
- **Live photograph** - Captured during the UPSC application using the current device webcam or by scanning the displayed QR code and using a mobile device. Good lighting and clear face required. The live image is matched with the uploaded passport photograph; mismatch blocks further application.
- **Triple-signature image** - Sign three times vertically, one below another, on plain white paper using black ink. Scan all three signatures in one clear, correctly oriented image. JPG; filename 'signature.jpg'; 20-100 KB; published image dimension 350-500 pixels.
- **Application declaration** - Candidate confirms the correctness of the application before final submission. No separate uploaded declaration file was found in the common instruction.

# 5. Cross-examination patterns

1. **UPSC now requires three distinct image actions:** an uploaded passport photograph, a mandatory live photograph, and one image containing three vertically arranged signatures.
2. **SSC applications centre on OTR plus live capture:** identity/OTP registration, a live photograph, a small signature image, and a disability certificate only for candidates claiming the relevant benefit.
3. **Banking and insurance recruitment commonly uses a four-image set:** photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration; recent IBPS cycles also require a Class 10 certificate and a scribe certificate where applicable.
4. **NTA examinations differ materially:** NEET adds both-hand finger/thumb impressions and a Class X marksheet; CUET may add category, disability and result-awaiting certificates; JEE Main is lighter; JEE Advanced is certificate-heavy for conditional eligibility routes.
5. **GATE has the clearest document-upload set:** photo, signature, photo ID, and conditional SC/ST, PwD/UDID and dyslexia certificates.
6. **State PSC and CET systems are the largest evidence gap:** the photograph rule is often published separately, while signature and claim-certificate requirements sit inside the active application portal or post-specific notification.

# 6. Research gaps and conflicts

- The supplied foundational report is photograph-specific. It provides strong field-level photo evidence but not complete signature/certificate evidence for every examination.
- SSC CGL 2026 contains conflicting signature dimensions in two sections (about 6.0 x 2.0 cm versus 4.0 x 2.0 cm). The active upload validator should be treated as operationally final.
- Several state PSC, CAT, KCET and WBJEE photo values are secondary-only or mixed-source. They are retained as leads, not upgraded into official rules.
- Some current portals expose a requirement only after login. Those items are labelled portal-dependent or not found rather than guessed.
- Physical document-verification lists, counselling documents, interview certificates and examination-centre carry lists are outside the report unless the item must also be uploaded during registration.

# 8. Final candidate-preparation master checklist

Before opening any examination portal, the candidate should first identify the exact examination cycle and then prepare only the items that its individual section marks mandatory or applicable. Across the covered systems, the practical master set is:

- A recent compliant digital photograph and, where required, a device capable of official live capture.
- A handwritten signature prepared exactly as the examination specifies.
- White paper and black/blue ink for any required thumb impression or handwritten declaration.
- Class X certificate/marksheet and other qualification proof where the portal asks for a scan.
- Aadhaar or another accepted photo identity document, with name and date-of-birth details matching the application.
- Current category, EWS/OBC-NCL, disability/UDID, domicile, scribe or other conditional certificates only when claiming the corresponding benefit.
- Clear, uncropped, readable scans with the correct number of pages and no password protection where PDF upload is required.
- The exact live-portal declaration text; never reuse a declaration from another examination without checking.

> Final operational rule: where this report says **not found**, **conflicting**, **prior-cycle**, **secondary-only** or **portal-dependent**, the active examination portal must be checked before final submission.
