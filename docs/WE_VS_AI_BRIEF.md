## Direction contract

THESIS: Explain the value of a connected exam-first workflow while keeping the comparison fair and source-backed.
MODE: Persuade and Read; exam applicants comparing the service with AI-assisted DIY.
OWN-WORLD: Inherit the live code's paper/ink/signal palette, existing thirteen-cell Wordmark component, Big Shoulders display and Anek body. No brand redesign. PRODUCT.md and the old homepage surface brief contain stale counts, prices and logo notes; current implementation wins.
STORY: What applicants coordinate themselves, what preparation does, what identity preservation means, limitations, price, questions and sources.
FIRST VIEWPORT: WE vs AI with a concise whole-job argument, one exam-selection CTA and a requirements sheet. Long-form details use paired rows, not a wall of feature cards. On phones each comparison retains explicit column labels.
SIGNATURE: Paired requirement rows resolve into an exam-first journey; native disclosure questions provide detail without hiding the core claim.
FORM: Existing application-form world, owner-pinned; code-led scoped extension, no new world or random seed required.
FINISH: Light/dark and phone/desktop verification; keyboard disclosures, canonical and sitemap, truthful support and price boundaries. No backend changes or deployment in this task.
